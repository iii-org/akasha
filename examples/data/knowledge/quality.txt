Manufacturing quality control checks whether produced items meet defined requirements.
Inspection can combine human review, measurement equipment, and machine vision.
A vision system can flag visible defects, but its results must be validated against representative samples.
Teams record defect rates and investigate changes in raw materials, machine settings, and environmental conditions.
Traceability links each product batch to its inputs and production history.
Quality data and maintenance data can be compared to investigate whether equipment condition affects defect rates.

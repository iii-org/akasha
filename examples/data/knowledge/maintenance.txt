Predictive maintenance uses measurements and historical records to identify signs of equipment deterioration.
For example, a sustained increase in vibration can indicate that a machine bearing needs inspection.
A maintenance team investigates the alert and schedules a repair before an unexpected breakdown.
Planned repairs can reduce unplanned downtime, but the system does not guarantee that every failure will be detected.
False alarms and missed faults should be monitored. Engineers periodically validate alarm thresholds.
The approach depends on reliable sensors, contextual information, and a clear maintenance workflow.
